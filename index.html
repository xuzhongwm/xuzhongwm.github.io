CS @ Columbia.

Hi! I am a CS undergrad at Columbia University specializing in reinforcement learning, large language models, and agent design. Broadly, I am interested in how we can teach machines to learn and adapt in complex environments, as well as how they see and understand the world post training.

Research

Currently, I am conducting research in AI under the guidance of Prof. Haipeng Chen and Prof. Yanfu Zhang. Here are a few paths I am exploring:

  • Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning: Developing techniques to improve how models navigate through distinct objectives (e.g. accuracy, cost, and format) in reasoning, coding, and tool usage.
  • Knowledge Editing: Establishing frameworks to update model facts with an external RL-trained retriever.
  • LLM Unlearning Methodologies: Entity (concept) removal from the LLM to eliminate safety and privacy concerns.

Beyond my core focus in machine learning, I also worked in pure mathematics and low-level systems. Under the guidance of Prof. Gexin Yu and Prof. Daniel W. Cranston, I conduct theoretical research on graph coloring and linear list-recoloring configurations. I also have explored the systems architecture and simulation under Prof. Yifan Sun, specifically contributing to the Akita framework in the data recording package.
