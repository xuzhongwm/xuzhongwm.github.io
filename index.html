About

I'm an undergraduate researcher working across machine learning and mathematics. At the D3i Lab, I design multi-objective reinforcement learning methods for reliable and specific in-context knowledge editing, and I'm currently investigating robust LLM unlearning. My honors thesis in graph theory develops new discharging techniques for bounding recoloring sequences under sparsity constraints. I've also contributed to Akita, an open-source computer architecture simulator, and worked as a machine learning engineer intern building LLM agent pipelines for document analysis.

Undergraduate Honors Thesis, Graph Theory

March 2025 – May 2026

Advisors: Daniel W. Cranston, Gexin Yu

  • Introduced the Generalized Extension Lemma, strengthening the Extension Lemma for bounding recoloring sequences from a single vertex to arbitrary subgraphs.
  • Developed a global discharging argument that exploits global graph structure, extending beyond classical local discharging to obtain sharper density bounds.
  • Proved that 5-list recoloring has linear diameter for mad(G) < 3 − ε; improved the linear bound for 6-list recoloring from 3.4 to 3.75 − ε.
