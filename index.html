Research

My work centers on multi-objective reinforcement learning for reliable, specific in-context knowledge editing, with an emphasis on avoiding reward hacking — and, more recently, on robust methods for LLM unlearning. In parallel, my mathematics thesis develops new discharging techniques for bounding recoloring sequences in sparse graphs, improving known bounds for list recoloring.
